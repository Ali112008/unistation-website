UK Non-Medical University Admission Packages 
These packages are designed for students applying to non-medical schools in the UK 
 
1- Basic Package ‘Application Ready’ 
- 
University Shortlisting & Selection 
We'll take you through the process of choosing the most suitable UK University for 
your academic profile, personal interests, and future career goals. 
- 
Personal Statement  
Assistance in crafting persuasive and effective personal statements that showcase 
your strengths. 
- 
University Admission:  
Complete assistance with application forms, document submission, and follow-up. 
- 
Interview Preparation 
One-to-one mock interviews for the traditional panel interviews. We'll conduct mock

-

2. Premium Package ‘Full University Admission Support’ 
- University Shortlisting & Selection: Extensive University Consultation: We'll take 
you through the process of choosing the most suitable UK University for your 
academic profile, personal interests, and future career goals. 
- IELTS Exam Preparation: Serious, intensive preparation with practice tests, criticism, 
and recommendations to prepare the student with all the information required to take 
the IELTS exam. 
- Personal Statement & Application Guidance: Personal Statement Coaching: 
Detailed feedback and coaching to help you write an effective personal statement that 
reflects your motivation for your study program, academic abilities, and extra-
curricular activities related to the career you are aspiring to have. 
- UCAS Application Support: Assistance with completing and submitting the UCAS 
application to ensure that your application is strong, mistake-free, and meets all the 
criteria. 
- Interview Preparation: Panel Interview Coaching: One-to-one mock interviews for 
the traditional panel interviews. We'll conduct mock interviews, providing 
constructive feedback and guidance to allow you to perform with confidence. 
- Profile Building: Assisting in the identification of pertinent extracurricular activities 
that will make your application stronger, for example, volunteering at organizations, 
internships, or conducting field-related research. Plus, suggestions on how to acquire 
hands-on experience, either through company/factory internships, research internships, 
or social work in the community. Guidance on how to cultivate imperative skills and 
hold leadership positions in applicable clubs or volunteer groups, demonstrating your 
initiative and leadership capabilities. Tips on how to properly document and present 
your experiences highlighting the skills and qualities most valued by UK universities 
(e.g., teamwork, empathy, problem-solving, communication). 
- Visa Application Guidance: Tier 4 visa application support, including 
documentation, visa interview preparation, and submission. 
- Pre-Departure Guidance: Pre-departure training to ensure a smooth transition into 
UK life, including familiarization with the healthcare system, accommodation, and 
student life necessities. Help with securing student accommodation close to your 
university. 
- Ongoing Support throughout the Process: From start to finish, we provide ongoing 
support via email and phone consultations to ensure that you're on the right path with 
each component of the application process, and beyond.
