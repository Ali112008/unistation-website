US University Admissions Packages 
 
1. Basic Package: "Application Ready" 
This package provides the essential support to get students into the university of their dream 
Included in the Basic Package: 
- 
University Shortlisting & University Selection: Inclusive, detailed university 
selection based on goals, budget, and interests. 
- 
Documents Preparation & Review: In-depth assistance with preparing, reviewing, 
and submitting all the documents required for university admissions. 
- 
Essay & Motivation Letter Writing Guidance: Basic coaching on how to write 
personal statements and motivation letters. 
- 
University Admission: Complete assistance with application forms, document 
submission, and follow-up. 
- 
Accommodation Arrangement Assistance: Personal help in organizing 
accommodation near the university. 
- 
Visa Application Guidance: Full support with preparing and submitting student visa 
applications. 
Ideal for: Students who need essential support for the application process and some 
guidance for their IELTS preparation. 
 
2. Standard Package: "University Admission Essentials" 
The Standard Package is suitable for students who need to receive necessary, focused 
guidance to enable them to successfully get into the university of their dream.  
 
 
 
The Standard Package comprises: 
- 
University Shortlisting & University Selection: Inclusive, detailed university 
selection based on goals, budget, and interests. 
- 
Documents Preparation & Review: In-depth assistance with preparing, reviewing, 
and submitting all the documents required for university admissions. 
- 
Essay & Motivation Letter Writing Guidance: Assistance in crafting persuasive and 
effective personal statements and motivation letters that showcase the student’s 
strengths. 
- 
IELTS Exam Preparation: Serious, intensive preparation with practice tests, 
criticism, and recommendations to prepare the student with all the information 
required to take the IELTS exam. 
- 
University Admission: Complete assistance with application forms, document 
submission, and follow-up. 
- 
Interview Preparation: Multiple mock interviews with constructive criticism and 
improvement suggestions. 
- 
Visa Application Guidance: Full support with preparing and submitting student visa 
applications. 
- 
Accommodation Arrangement Assistance: Personal help in organizing 
accommodation near the university. 
 
Ideal for: 
Students seeking the budget-friendly plan with standard services to assist them through the 
application process for a university. Students who need minimum assistance with regard to 
university selection, documents, and exam readiness would be suitable for this package.
